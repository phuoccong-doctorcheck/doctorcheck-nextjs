## 2. Global SEO & Indexing Rules

1. **URL Permalink Architecture:**
   - Strict trailing slash enforcement on all pages (e.g., `https://www.doctorcheck.vn/bang-gia-dich-vu-tam-soat-benh-tai-doctor-check/`).
   - Clean, descriptive slugs in Vietnamese without diacritics.
2. **Robots Directives:**
   - Global: `follow, index, max-snippet:-1, max-video-preview:-1, max-image-preview:large`
   - Disallow directives in `robots.txt`:
     - `/wp-content/uploads/wc-logs/`
     - `/wp-content/uploads/woocommerce_transient_files/`
     - `/wp-content/uploads/woocommerce_uploads/`
3. **OpenGraph & Social Meta:**
   - Consistent `og:locale = vi_VN`, `og:type = website` or `article`.
   - High-resolution OpenGraph banners (`og:image`) predominantly served in WebP format with dimensions `1200x630` or `660x422`.
4. **Heading Hierarchy Standards:**
   - Every page features exactly one primary `<h1>` tag corresponding to the page's core keyword intent.
   - Structured `<h2>` and `<h3>` subheadings segment services, doctor rosters, and FAQs.
